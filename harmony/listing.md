## Game title (max 80)
Soccer Shot

## Description (max 2000)
Tap to shoot. Five goals, three misses, one sliding striker.

Your striker slides back and forth along the goal line. Tap to shoot, and time it so the ball lands in any of the five goals.

Score back-to-back goals to build a combo for double points. Chain 10 in a row and the goals catch fire. Miss three times and it's full time.

Controls: tap anywhere on mobile. On desktop, press Space, Enter, W or Up Arrow.
